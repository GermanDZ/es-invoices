# T-009 — Design / decision notes

Working notes behind `docs/pricing-validation.md` (PRICE-001). The artifact is the
deliverable; this records *why* it is shaped as it is.

## DD1 — Why a hypothesis + validation plan, not a finished WTP study

Real willingness-to-pay can only come from target users (interviews + a beta
cohort paying real money). The analyst cannot manufacture that data. The
honest, OpenUP-correct move in Elaboration is therefore to (a) reduce R-04 with
the evidence that *is* obtainable now — competitor benchmarks, segment context,
funnel math — and (b) hand the founder a concrete, verifiable validation plan
(§6) that converts the hypothesis to measured fact before heavy spend. That
plan *is* R-04's pre-agreed mitigation, so authoring it discharges the risk's
mitigation obligation rather than restating the risk.

## DD2 — Why founder-decision, not analyst-decision

Price point, free-tier vs trial, and the committed paying-accounts target trade
the founder's revenue model and cash/runway. Per the same logic that made AD-5/
AD-6 (stack) and AD-3 (build-vs-buy, T-007) founder calls, these are
founder-only inputs. The lane raises an input-request and **suspends** (exit via
handoff), rather than the analyst deciding. Mirrors the T-007 precedent exactly.

## DD3 — Recommendation rationale (single flat ~7€ + trial; free tier only if deadline defers)

- "One job, done well" (scope §4) argues against tiering (Model C) in v1.
- A near Verifactu deadline makes demand urgency-driven → a clean paid plan +
  trial converts on compliance fear without a standing free-tier cost.
- A *deferred* deadline (the 2 Dec 2025 prórroga to 2027) softens urgency → a
  capped free tier (Model B) to capture users early becomes the stronger play.
  Hence the recommendation is **conditional on the deadline** (Q5), not absolute.
- Benchmark band 6–13 €/mo (competitor entry plans) places ~7€ at/just below the
  low end — the simplicity+price wedge of R-05's mitigation.

## DD4 — Deadline uncertainty is load-bearing and unresolved

RD 254/2025 set the autónomo date at 1 Jul 2026; a 2 Dec 2025 announcement
proposed deferring it to 1 Jul 2027. As of 2026-06-15 which governs is
unconfirmed. This swings both the go-to-market calendar and the free-tier
decision, so it is surfaced as founder steering input (Q5) and cross-linked to
R-01 (regulatory timing), not silently assumed.

## DD5 — Target derived from funnel, not picked

The paying-accounts target is computed as signups × activation (Vision ≥50%) ×
trial→paid (unknown, to be measured). The artifact gives an illustrative
~150–400 band so the founder ratifies a *funnel-consistent* number rather than a
guess; the band narrows once beta conversion is read.
